tips

how to use

every item must have a title and body. category defaults to script; tags can be an array or a comma-separated string. titles are capped at 80 characters, bodies at 32 KB, and tags at 10 entries × 24 chars.

items missing required fields are skipped and reported back. nothing is overwritten — every import creates new entries.
